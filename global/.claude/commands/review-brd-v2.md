Invoke the review-brd-v2 skill. Reviews a two-file BRD — docs/brd/[change-name]-BRD.md from /write-brd-v2 and its [change-name]-SR.md stakeholder requirements from /write-stakeholder — for readiness to proceed. It first detects the stage: no BRD (offers /write-brd-v2), BRD only (is it ready for /write-stakeholder?), or both files matched by the SR file's brd field (are they consistent, and ready together for ORD development?). It then checks each file against its authoring skill's verification gate and runs eight cross-document checks, including staleness against brd_version. It applies /review-brd's BH-1 – BH-10 gate, business definition readiness, acceptance derivability, readiness projection and path to readiness through a v2 section map, reading criteria from the requirements pack and never recalling them. It does not treat v2's lean layout as a defect. AFK advisory — per-item verdicts with evidence and derived outcomes, never a score; it edits no document. Use when /review-brd-v2 is run, or a v2 BRD needs a readiness check before stakeholder requirements or before ORD work is assigned.
