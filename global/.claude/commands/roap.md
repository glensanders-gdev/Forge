Invoke the roap skill. Grills a hiring manager into a complete Role on a Page — role title, team, reports to, role purpose, key accountabilities, success measures, typical activities with percentage allocation, development focus areas, and key relationships — then writes it to docs/roles/[role-slug]-roap.md. Nine questions asked one at a time, with a probe on any answer that describes a person rather than a position, offers tasks as accountabilities, or gives a measure with no metric; allocation must total exactly 100% before the page is drafted. Optional argument: the role title (e.g. /roap "Senior Business Analyst"). Use when a new role is being designed or an existing role needs documenting on one page.
