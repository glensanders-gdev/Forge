Invoke the review-brd skill. Assesses a submitted BRD against the requirements-documents pack on three independent questions: the handoff gate — the bar (BH-1 to BH-4), the supporting items (BH-5 to BH-10), the `[TBD]` treatment rule and its two limits, with one of four outcomes derived mechanically; business definition readiness (BD-1 to BD-10) — whether requirements can be derived without a downstream team inventing business policy, with one of four outcomes; and the acceptance criteria that cannot yet be safely derived. Then projects the ORD's lead time, maturity tier and confidence from the BRD's condition, the stakeholder count and the allocation, drafting RAID entries where confidence is Low. Never resolves a business decision on the business's behalf. Where the gate outcome is *Not yet ready for ORD development*, records it rather than exercising it, because the authority it depends on is a Tier 1 control the standard states is not yet held. Criteria are read from the pack at review time, never recalled; where the pack cannot be located the review stops rather than proceeding from memory. Pass the BRD as a file path or in the conversation, with the allocation and fixed date if known. Use when a BRD arrives for ORD development, or before ORD work is assigned against one.
